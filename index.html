About the Project

Early design, without repeated CFD

This project develops an integrated, machine learning based conceptual-design tool for small UAVs, combining CFD-derived airfoil data, an in-house Extra-Trees + Gaussian Process surrogate for NACA 4-digit polars, and first-pass flight sizing. It reduces the need for repeated CFD runs during early design and makes mission-based airfoil selection accessible to non-specialists.

Everything runs in your browser. Open the page, choose a mission, and go from airfoil selection to a first-pass performance estimate in minutes.

Key Innovations

Built for non-specialists, transparent for engineers

Mission-driven ranking

Pick surveillance, mapping, cargo, racing, reconnaissance or custom, and airfoils are ranked with transparent, adjustable weights for efficiency, lift and stall safety.

In-house ML polar predictor

An Extra-Trees + Gaussian Process model written from scratch predicts lift and drag polars for NACA 4-digit airfoils instantly in your browser.

Airfoil to aircraft

The chosen polar feeds directly into wing geometry, Reynolds number, drag, power, battery current, endurance and range.

Atmosphere-aware

Density, viscosity and Reynolds number come from the ISA atmosphere at your design altitude.

Platform-aware defaults

Built-in factors for eight platform types and a VTOL energy budget, so you never need to guess values like Oswald efficiency.

Score = w1·Cl/Cd + w2·Clmax + w3·α

Fully transparent

Every score and result follows a published formula you can inspect.

Accessible to everyone

No subscriptions, licences, installation, solver setup or CFD expertise required.

Module D — NACA Wind Tunnel Simulator

Real-time 2D flow visualisation (Lattice-Boltzmann D2Q9) around the NACA sections of your Verified Library.

The tunnel runs at a low lattice Reynolds number, so its live Cl/Cd are for qualitative flow visualisation. Quantitative values come from the Verified Library CFD polars (Re ≈ 300,000) shown above.
